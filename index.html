01 — Our Story

Yuli carries the memory.
Her team carries the craft.

Yuli grew up making pindang the way her family always made it — with tomat ranti, a small sour tomato her mother added to the broth that you won't find in any recipe book. She still adds it. It's the difference between pindang and her pindang.

Around her stands a culinary team with many years of experience across restaurants, private dining and luxury resorts — the organisation and precision of a professional kitchen, placed in service of flavours that began in a family home. What arrives at your table was never fusion. It is a translation of European style with Indonesian soul.

Family recipes, carried by a team that has cooked them for years.

I · Table d'Hôte

Chef Nic's first table d'hôte was in Provence in 1997, at a farmhouse surrounded by land where truffles were found.

One table, generous food, great wine and people gathering around whatever the host chose to serve.

Years later in Asia, omakase felt instantly familiar.

Yunic brings that same spirit to your home: one table, one menu, seasonal produce, and the pleasure of simply trusting what comes next.

II

II · Rijsttafel

When Chef Nic arrived in Bali, Sri Owen's book introduced him to the spirit of rijsttafel: a table brought to life through many different dishes, each carrying its own flavour, story and sense of place.

It felt familiar, generous, shared and built around discovery.

At Yunic, Yuli and Nic bring that table to you, creating a journey through Indonesian flavours, memories and traditions, all made to be shared.

III · Party Events

Yunic likes seated dinners small, personal and intimate. For bigger celebrations, we let the food move with the party.

Refined canapés and small bites for pool parties, home parties, after-parties and celebrations, designed to be eaten easily in one or two bites.

From caviar to Indonesian snacks: no buffet, no oversized plates, no smoky barbecue.

Just thoughtful food that keeps moving while you do.

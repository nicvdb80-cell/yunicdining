Our Story

Yuli, Nic and the idea behind Yunic.

Yunic grew from Yuli and Nic’s shared life in Bali and a wish to create something of their own. It brings together her Indonesian warmth, his Belgian roots and the pleasure they both find in gathering people around good food.

Yuli, founder and owner, looks after the hosting, organisation and details that make guests feel welcome. Nic leads the culinary direction. His Indonesian journey began at CasCades in 2013, before helping bring Apéritif Ubud to life, shaping the culinary concept and identity that became central to the restaurant.

With Yunic, they have room to explore together, inspired by their travels, their love of the ocean and the people they meet along the way.

Something of their world, brought into yours.

The Yunic Approach

Your occasion, our inspiration.

An intimate dinner, an Indonesian rijsttafel, a canapé reception or a culinary collaboration at a boutique dive resort. You might have a celebration in mind, a favourite ingredient in season, or simply a wish to bring people together over good food.

The menu can be produce-driven, rooted in Indonesian flavours or eclectic and inspired by our travels. Whatever the occasion, our approach stays the same: less is more. Every ingredient has a purpose, allowing the produce and flavours to shine.

Tell us what you have in mind. Together, we find the direction that suits your occasion and what Yunic does best.

The Yunic Kitchen

It starts with what we want to cook—and what you love to eat.

Good produce gives us the starting point. Experience helps us decide what to do with it.

Our menus draw on European techniques, Indonesian flavours and ideas gathered along the way. Some dishes are familiar, others take a different direction. Each needs to earn its place on the menu.

Have a favourite ingredient? Tell us. It might be where your Yunic experience begins.

Dive & Dine

An idea born between the ocean and the table.

Diving and cooking have taken Nic to some remarkable places. Yunic Dive & Dine brings those two passions together.

For boutique dive resorts, liveaboards and yachts, we develop sharing menus, à la carte menus, set menus and romantic dinners for two. Through pop-ups and collaborations, we share recipes and techniques with your chefs to bring the idea to life at your destination.

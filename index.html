Our Story

The people behind Yunic

Yunic Hospitality was founded by Yuli, with Nic leading its culinary direction.

Yuli is the founder and owner, looking after the organisation and the details behind each experience. Nic brings his European culinary background, years of cooking in Indonesia and a curiosity shaped by travel, ingredients and the people he meets.

Together with a small team, they bring Yunic into places where people want to gather, eat well and feel at ease.

The cooking is eclectic and personal. A menu might begin with Indonesian flavours, a French classic, something discovered while travelling or simply an ingredient you love.

We choose the experiences we take on carefully, so we can give each one the attention it deserves.

II · Rijsttafel

A generous spread of Indonesian dishes, served together for everyone to explore.

The Yunic Kitchen

It starts with what we want to cook—and what you love to eat.

Good produce gives us the starting point. Experience helps us decide what to do with it.

Our menus draw on European techniques, Indonesian flavours and ideas gathered along the way. Some dishes are familiar, others take a different direction. Each needs to earn its place on the menu.

Have a favourite ingredient? Tell us. It might be where your Yunic experience begins.
